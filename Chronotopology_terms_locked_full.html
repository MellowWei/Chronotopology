Abstract · English

Modern vocabularies of time and space often reduce them to physical coordinates, measurable dimensions, or abstract backgrounds, leaving underdeveloped the question of how existence is lived as both continuation and standing. Chronotopology proposes a philosophical-ontological research artifact that integrates Time-ology and Space-ology as two orthogonal phases of one source-field vibration. Its approach is axiomatic and linguistic-phenomenological: seven time axioms define time as flow, continuation, imperative, practice-event, and phenomenological physical fact; seven space axioms define space as sentence-event, necessary linehood within time, no-directionality, standing-fast, void-space-ness, crossability, and reversal. The contribution is a fourteen-axiom framework that distinguishes horizontal -ing (time) from vertical -ing (space), then maps their non-bijective correspondence through a Time–Space bridge table. Chronotopology is not a replacement for physics, geometry, topology, or relativity. It is a philosophical-ontological research artifact about how time and space appear as continuation and standing in lived source-field experience, prepared for later citation, comparative theory work, and academic formalization.

Ontological / philosophical scope · 本体论 / 哲学范围

Chronotopology is a philosophical discipline of time-as-flow and space-as-void, not a physics theory. Statements like "time is flowing", "tomorrow is the continuing physical fact of time", or "void = space-ness" are phenomenological-ontological formulations. They are not empirical claims competing with general relativity, thermodynamics, quantum mechanics, or geometric topology.

时空学是关于时间—作为—流动和空间—作为—虚无的哲学学科,不是物理学理论。“时间是流动的”“明天是时间的继续物理事实”“虚无 = 空间性”等表述是现象学—本体论表述。它们不是与广义相对论、热力学、量子力学或几何拓扑学竞争的经验主张。

"Continue" is not "overwork" · 继续不是过度劳动

"Continue" as the imperative of time names the structural fact that time goes on — not a demand to push through exhaustion, suppress rest, or override the body. Continuation includes rest, sleep, mourning, stillness. "To walk a stretch" (T-Ω+2) explicitly includes the stretch where one sits down. "Standing" (S-3) explicitly includes the standing that just is — not productive standing, just being-here.

作为时间律令的“继续”命名时间持续这一结构性事实——不是一道要求一个人推开疲惫、压抑休息、覆盖身体的命令。继续包含休息、睡眠、哀悼与静止。“走一程”(T-Ω+2)明确包括坐下来的那一程。“站住”(S-3)明确包括只是在这儿的站立——不是有产出的站立,只是在这里。

"Boundary" is structural, not social · 边界是结构,不是社会建构

Space-ology's S-Ω+1 (the crossability of a boundary implies time-space has both physicality and morality) provides ontological grounding for what is, in lived experience, often dismissed as "oversensitivity." This discipline takes the position that: those who already recognize a boundary do not need to be taught its existence — and those who repeatedly cross boundaries cannot rewrite the structure by claiming "it wasn't really a boundary."

空间学 S-Ω+1(越界的可能性代表时空有物理性和道德性)为生活经验中常被消解为"过敏"的事提供本体论凭证。本学科的立场是:已经识别到边界的人不需要被教导边界存在——而反复越界的人也不能通过宣称"那不算边界"来重写时空结构。
